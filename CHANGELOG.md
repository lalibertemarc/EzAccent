# EzAccent

## Unreleased

- **Say it like you mean it.** `/acc <text>` says your line in your character's accent: orc, troll, dwarf or undead (a locked jaw). Orcs, trolls, dwarves and Forsaken start in their own; anyone can pick one with `/acc accent`. `/acc thick on` lays it on thicker: more Orcish, heavier troll and Scots, Scottish Gaelic for dwarves, and undead lose their jaw altogether.
- **Any channel.** `/acc y`, `p`, `g`, `o`, `r` and `e` yell, party, guild, officer, raid or emote the line (an emote with "quoted speech" only accents the quotes); `/acc w <name> <text>` whispers it (your target's first name fills in their surname).
- **A box of your own.** A key binding (Options > Keybindings > EzAccent) or `/acc box` opens a small edit box: Enter says the line, a leading `/y`, `/p`, `/w name`... picks the channel, Escape closes it.
- **Jawless means jawless.** With no lower teeth to hiss against, a jawless undead's th and s sounds come out slushy too ("Yusht shtay shtill"), on top of the missing lip sounds, and now and then a wet sound slips out mid-line: "Hhleashe, shlrk, ghe quiet."
- **A jaw that won't open.** The locked-jaw undead now squeezes the long i too ("I leyke my teyme elune", "feyt"), cramps mid-line now and then ("Hellu my friend, nngh, I'm guing..."), and sometimes catches on a word's first sound ("H-huw heve yuu been?").
- **Thick orcs talk like grunts.** Less "Zug zug" on every line (a closer now comes 4 times in 10, with "Gol'kosh!" for fighting talk), and more of the line changes: "Me need help with this quest.", "You ready? We must go now.", "Where inn? Me need grog.", "Pinkskins come." Questions and "was/will/be" lose their helper verbs. Fixed "The humans are coming" becoming "Humans comes".
- **Thick trolls go full Darkspear.** The loa no longer gets the last word on every line (4 or 5 times in 10 now, with new closers like "Stay away from da voodoo." and "Bwonsamdi be waitin' for dem." for fighting talk), ", mon" comes a bit less often, and more of the line changes: "Ya wanna come wit' us ta da dungeon?", "Ya be ready?", "Me be huntin' in da forest", "Tank ya plenty", "we oughta wait fa da others".
- **Thick dwarves speak broad Scots.** Not every line ends in "Seadh." anymore (half the lines without Gaelic get some, with "By my beard!" for fighting talk and "So it is." among the closers), "Och!" and ", lad" come less often, and more of the line changes: "Ah wis huntin' in the forest", "Whaur is the inn?", "D'ye want tae come?", "We hae tae go the noo", "Mìle taing, that wis awfy kind o' ye", plus richt, nicht, gaun, a'body, naethin', eejit. Light dwarves say lass and laddie, and "Hello there" is just "Hullo".
- **Try before you speak.** `/acc test <text>` prints a few versions in your own chat and sends nothing.
- Chat links, raid marks like `{skull}` and `%t` come through untouched, and long lines are split at the 255-character chat limit (the extras go first).
