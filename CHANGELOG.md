# EzAccent

## Unreleased

- **Whisper from EzAcquaintances.** Its book's Whisper button can open this box already on a whisper to that person (right-click the button and pick "In your accent").
- **Long lines no longer get stuck.** The game lets an addon say only one message per key press in say, yell, emotes and channels, so the second piece of a line too long for one message was blocked ("tried to call the protected function"). Now the first piece goes out and the box opens with the rest waiting: press Enter for each (or `/acc more`), Escape drops them. Party, raid, guild, officer and whispers still get every piece at once.
- **Orcs sound like orcs.** Fewer broken lines and more Durotar: "Thanks, {name}!" becomes "Aka'Magosh, {name}!" but "my family thanks you" stays a thanks ("my clan thanks you"), "Isn't time funny?" no longer turns into "Does not time funny?", "Lovely day, isn't it?" asks "Good day, yes?", "I've got a plan" keeps its "have", and "you're a dream" keeps its "a". Filler goes ("I'm so sorry to interrupt, but could I possibly" → "sorry, but can I"), birds are vultures, pie is roast boar, a black cat is a scorpid, "I've done the math" counts on its fingers, and lines open with "Throm-ka." now and then and end on a word that fits: Aka'Magosh for a thanks, Lok'tar ogar or Blood and thunder for a fight, Bah for bad news, Dabu? for a question. Thick orcs still say "You ready?" and "Me hungry", but keep the verb after anything else ("your {buff} is as good as your eyes"), keep "should have" (no more "you must have given us"), and keep "an hour ago" and "a moment" whole.
- **Thick trolls nag less.** Their loa closers come on fewer lines, and "Stay away from da voodoo." no longer lands on lines it has nothing to do with.
- **Ellipses stay.** "I, um... {buff}?" no longer loses two of its dots in any accent, and no interjection is added in front of one that's already there ("Och! Oh, ...").
- **Lad or lass.** A woman you talk to is now a "lass", not a "lad", and a thick troll calls her "sistah", not "bruddah": your target in say, yell and emotes (party and raid when she's in your group), or whoever you whisper (known from your target, your group, or her whispers). Unknown listeners, guild and officer chat keep "lad", words you typed yourself are never changed, and `/acc test` shows the lines for your target.
- **Say it like you mean it.** `/acc <text>` says your line in your character's accent: orc, troll, dwarf or undead (a locked jaw). Orcs, trolls, dwarves and Forsaken start in their own; anyone can pick one with `/acc accent`. `/acc thick on` lays it on thicker: more Orcish, heavier troll and Scots, Scottish Gaelic for dwarves, and undead lose their jaw altogether.
- **Any channel.** `/acc y`, `p`, `g`, `o`, `r` and `e` yell, party, guild, officer, raid or emote the line (an emote with "quoted speech" only accents the quotes); `/acc w <name> <text>` whispers it (the first name of your target, someone you whispered with or someone in your group fills in their surname).
- **A box of your own.** A key binding (Options > Keybindings > EzAccent) or `/acc box` opens a small edit box. Enter says the line, Escape closes it. The channel shows at the start of the line, as in chat (`Say:`, `Tell Jaina Proudmoore:`), and the box stays on it: type `/y`, `/p`, `/e`... and a space to switch, or `/w bob ` to whisper (his surname filled in when the box knows him: your target, the people you whispered with, your group). Tab whispers the next of those people, a click on the channel lists them all with the channels, and Up brings back a line with its channel.
- **Jawless means jawless.** With no lower teeth to hiss against, a jawless undead's th and s sounds come out slushy too ("Yusht shtay shtill"), on top of the missing lip sounds, and now and then a wet sound slips out mid-line: "Hhleashe, shlrk, ghe quiet."
- **Jawless to jawless.** Two jawless undead understand each other: EzAccent sends what you meant along with your line (an addon message, unseen), and another EzAccent user who is undead thick reads it after the slush, in grey ("Yusht shtay shtill. Ghk. (Just stay still.)"). Everyone else, with EzAccent or not, only hears the slush. Say, yell, emote quotes, party, raid, guild, officer and whispers; a meaning that comes in after its line shows on a line of its own.
- **A jaw that won't open.** The locked-jaw undead now squeezes the long i too ("I leyke my teyme elune", "feyt"), cramps mid-line now and then ("Hellu my friend, nngh, I'm guing..."), and sometimes catches on a word's first sound ("H-huw heve yuu been?").
- **Thick orcs talk like grunts.** Less "Zug zug" on every line (a closer now comes 4 times in 10, with "Gol'kosh!" for fighting talk), and more of the line changes: "Me need help with this quest.", "You ready? We must go now.", "Where inn? Me need grog.", "Pinkskins come." Questions and "was/will/be" lose their helper verbs. Fixed "The humans are coming" becoming "Humans comes".
- **Thick trolls go full Darkspear.** The loa no longer gets the last word on every line (4 or 5 times in 10 now, with new closers like "Stay away from da voodoo." and "Bwonsamdi be waitin' for dem." for fighting talk), ", mon" comes a bit less often, and more of the line changes: "Ya wanna come wit' us ta da dungeon?", "Ya be ready?", "Me be huntin' in da forest", "Tank ya plenty", "we oughta wait fa da others".
- **Thick dwarves speak broad Scots.** Not every line ends in "Seadh." anymore (half the lines without Gaelic get some, with "By my beard!" for fighting talk and "So it is." among the closers), "Och!" and ", lad" come less often, and more of the line changes: "Ah wis huntin' in the forest", "Whaur is the inn?", "D'ye want tae come?", "We hae tae go the noo", "Mìle taing, that wis awfy kind o' ye", plus richt, nicht, gaun, a'body, naethin', eejit. Light dwarves say lass and laddie, and "Hello there" is just "Hullo".
- **An options page.** Esc > Options > AddOns > EzAccent (or `/acc config`): pick this character's accent, lay it on thick, preview a sample line, open the box or put it back in its first spot, and jump to its key binding.
- **One accent for all your addons.** Changing your accent or thick in EzAccent (`/acc accent`, `/acc thick` or the options page) also sets it in Dead But Delicious and Buff Me Please, when they're loaded, to the nearest one each has: undead is their locked jaw, undead thick is jawless, and any other accent turns Dead But Delicious' off (it only speaks undead). Their own accent commands still change theirs alone. Switch it off with `/acc share off` or on the options page.
- **Try before you speak.** `/acc test <text>` prints a few versions in your own chat and sends nothing.
- Chat links, raid marks like `{skull}` and `%t` come through untouched, and long lines are split at the 255-character chat limit (the extras go first).
